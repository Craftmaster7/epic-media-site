Our story

Built for the rooms where communities gather.

Some ideas begin with a vision. This one began with a table. Not the product, the moment: the unhurried stretch between ordering and the check, the way people settle into a restaurant, drop their shoulders, and stay awhile. Scott T. recognized something in that pause that most of the advertising industry was too distracted to notice: sustained, willing attention from people who chose to be there. It was 2008. He built a company around it.

David B. had been thinking the same thing for years, with his own restaurants, his own tables, and his own proof that physical presence in the right room is worth more than a thousand impressions on a screen. When they formalized their partnership in 2019, it felt less like a business decision and more like finishing a sentence someone else had started.

Today Epic Media Networks works with independent restaurants across America, providing custom tabletop installations at no cost, designed to feel like they belong there. Local businesses earn a presence inside the rooms where their neighbors already choose to gather. No algorithms. No impressions that expire at midnight.
