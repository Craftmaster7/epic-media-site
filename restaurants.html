Common questions

What's the catch? There isn't one.

What does it actually cost my restaurant?

Nothing. Not at installation, not monthly, and not at the two-year refresh. Since 2008 we have never billed a restaurant for the program.

Who designs the tables?

Our design team builds the artwork around your restaurant's brand and atmosphere. You review the proof and approve it before anything prints.

What am I agreeing to?

A simple 2-year display agreement: keep the tables on your floor for the program period. That's the whole ask.

What happens after two years?

We refresh the tables with a new design and new local advertisers, again at no cost, and the cycle begins again.

Where do the advertisers come from?

Local businesses near your restaurant. Each one approves its own ad before it prints. Ask us on the call about how placements around your table are handled.
